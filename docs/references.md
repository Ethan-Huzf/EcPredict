# Method references

- Ma, J. et al. *Intermolecular 3D-MoRSE Descriptors for Fast and Accurate Prediction of Electronic Couplings in Organic Semiconductors*. Journal of Chemical Information and Modeling **2023**, 63, 5089–5096.
- Li, X. et al. *A set of optimized 3D-MoRSE descriptors for molecular representation*. npj Computational Materials **2026**. Related code: https://github.com/Leonardo-Lixp/OPT3D.
- Bai, X.; Guo, X.; Wang, L. *Machine Learning Approach to Calculate Electronic Couplings between Quasi-diabatic Molecular Orbitals: The Case of DNA*. Journal of Physical Chemistry Letters **2021**, 12, 10457–10464.

EcPredict's seven-weight pentacene descriptor implementation is derived from the local distance-PKL converter and its C/H weight constants, as recorded in `source_manifest.json`. The related OPT3D repository is a reference implementation for a different range of molecular-property tasks. Its datasets, model scope, environment and license should not be assumed to apply automatically to this code-only archive.
